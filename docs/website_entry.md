## Summary
A local research bench demonstrating how thin-liquidity DEX token markets can be distorted by coordinated activity, apparent volume, and liquidity shocks. The project is designed to communicate why public on-chain data does not automatically make a market fair, organic, or safe to trade.

## Abstract
DEX Market Fragility Lab is a local-only research project that models the lifecycle of a token market in a simplified automated market maker environment. It uses synthetic wallets, simulated liquidity operations, price-impact calculations, and event logging to show how shallow decentralized markets can become highly sensitive to coordinated actions. The project was created to demonstrate how easily DEX token markets can be manipulated or misread, especially when liquidity is thin and apparent activity is mistaken for genuine demand. Its purpose is educational and analytical: to make the mechanics of market fragility visible, explain why decentralized markets can be structurally vulnerable, and show why trading in such environments can be unreliable and high-risk.

## Project blocks

### heading
DEX Market Fragility Lab

### text
This project is a controlled, local-only research bench for studying how token markets on decentralized exchanges can become fragile and easy to misread. It models a simplified AMM pool, synthetic wallets, apparent trading activity, liquidity changes, and price impact.

### text
The main idea is straightforward: transparency does not guarantee market integrity. Even when every transaction is visible on-chain, a low-liquidity market can still be distorted by coordinated behavior, shallow reserves, and misleading volume signals.

### quote
Public on-chain data can show everything that happened while still failing to prove that the market is organic, healthy, or safe.

### heading
Why it matters

### text
The project was created to show how easily decentralized token markets can be manipulated or misinterpreted. It illustrates an approximate process behind market distortion without connecting to real exchanges, deploying real tokens, or executing real trades.

### list
- Simulates a simplified constant-product AMM pool.
- Uses synthetic wallets instead of real accounts.
- Tracks price impact, apparent volume, liquidity depth, and event history.
- Demonstrates why thin-liquidity DEX markets are structurally vulnerable.
- Frames the result as a risk-awareness and research tool, not a trading system.

### heading
Scope

### text
The public version intentionally avoids live-chain execution. It contains no private keys, no RPC configuration, no deployment scripts, and no automated exchange interaction. The goal is to explain market fragility responsibly and make the risk legible for readers.
